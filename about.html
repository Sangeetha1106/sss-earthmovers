About Us

Who we are, what we believe in and how we work.

SSS – Your Trusted Partner

A team of seasoned professionals

SSS Earthmovers and Transports is a dynamic company formed by a team of seasoned professionals with extensive experience and expertise in the infrastructure sector. Our team comprises young, qualified business graduates and engineers from prestigious institutions, united by a shared commitment to excellence.

Our company philosophy is centred on building long-term partnerships with our clients. Our goal is to position ourselves as your top choice by providing a full spectrum of services that cater to every aspect of your construction and infrastructure needs.

From earthmoving and excavation to the supply of high-quality construction materials, we offer a seamless and integrated approach to project management. Our diverse range of services ensures that you have access to everything you need under one roof, simplifying your workflow and enhancing efficiency.
