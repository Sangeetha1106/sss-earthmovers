Questions

Frequently asked

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Vellore, Tamil Nadu and provide transport and equipment services across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Kerala and beyond – PAN India.

Can I hire machines without buying materials?

Yes. SSS Equipment Rentals offers excavators, JCB 3DX backhoe loaders, cranes, EOT cranes, tippers and more on hire, separately or along with material supply.

Which construction materials do you supply?

Zone 2 fine river sand, aggregates (10mm, 12mm, 20mm, 40mm, dust and M-sand), cement, steel, gravel, red soil, solid blocks, paving blocks, bricks, DBM and more.

Are you a government-approved supplier?

Yes – we are a government-approved supplier of gravel and aggregates in Vellore, recognised for adherence to quality standards, and we have executed work for DRDA, BDO offices, HR & CE and local bodies.
